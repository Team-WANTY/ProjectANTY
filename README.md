# Project ANTY (interim name)

**The feed that keeps you productive!**

Project ANTY is a new kind of productivity app that combines the essentials of task management with the engaging mechanics of social media and gamification. Whether you're tackling daily goals or teaming up with friends, Project ANTY makes getting things done fun and rewarding.

## Overview

Instead of being a solo grind, staying productive becomes a shared and interactive experience. In a world full of to-do list apps, Project ANTY stands apart by tapping into human connection and friendly competition. We're building a space where productivity is more than a checklist, it's a community. With built-in social features, collaborative task modes, and gamified incentives, users stay motivated through cooperation, competition, and customization.

---

## Core Features

### Productivity Tools
- Create and manage tasks with due dates, categories, and priorities
- Organize tasks into personal or shared lists
- Track your progress via easy-to-read analytics

### Social Layer
- Add your friends and strike things off your list together
- Post updates, share achievements, or even celebrate task completions
- Interact with others via likes, comments, and shareable task boards
- View user profiles to see analytics, badges, and activity

### Gamification & Rewards
- Earn points by completing tasks, collaborating, or competing!
- Redeem points for:
  - Cosmetic rewards (profile borders, bio backgrounds, name fonts, animated effects)
  - Social enhancers (super likes, custom emoji reactions, encouragement badges)
